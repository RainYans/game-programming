## In progress — Slice 4 (M3 P0 closeout)

The agent-side capabilities are already in `BattleAgent`:

- Behaviour priority Frozen → Flee → Commanded target → Commanded move → Default AI.
- Public API: `SetSelected`, `SetCommandTarget`, `SetMoveCommand`, `ClearCommands`, `Repel`,
  `Freeze`, `ApplyCorrosion`. Player units render an HP bar + selection ring.
- **All six passives implemented**: ThickHide, Bloodlust, Evasion, Corrosion, Aura,
  SelfDetonate (constants at top of `BattleAgent` — tune freely).
- `BattleCameraFollow.Shake(magnitude, duration)` available for hit / death feedback.

**What's still TBD for Slice 4 to be playable end-to-end:**

1. **Mouse input layer** — a `BattleController` that does left-click / box-select on player
   agents (calling `SetSelected`) and right-click to command (`SetCommandTarget` on an enemy
   under the cursor, else `SetMoveCommand` to the ground point). Needs the battle camera to
   resolve world points; the scene already has an EventSystem.
2. **Control items** — at minimum **Rotten Onion** (P0): an item the leader carries, used at
   the cursor on a number-key + left-click, that calls `enemy.Repel(center, distance, duration)`
   on every enemy in radius. Freeze Canister (calls `Freeze`) and Barbed Wire are P1.
   Inventory of items + a small HUD bar showing carried items.
3. **Wild-zombie variants** — `CitySetup` already expects assets named `Wild_Normal` /
   `Wild_Runner` / `Wild_Brute` (and falls back to the legacy `WildZombile` for "normal").
   Need a **Setup Wild Zombies** Editor menu that generates the three variants in
   `Assets/ScriptableObject/Wild/` so the mixed-stage city actually works.
4. **Battle scene wiring** — extend `Setup Battle Scene` to add the controller, item HUD, and
   any new refs the above need.
